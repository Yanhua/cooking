**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 200 g dried rice noodles
- 1 each broccoli
- 1 red capsicum
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 45 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Beef Rice Noodles,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/beef-rice-noodles/](https://www.recipetineats.com/beef-rice-noodles/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Nagi's explicit chicken option for the beef rice noodle stir-fry and scales it to two.
- Retains the soy–oyster–wine sauce and uses broccoli and capsicum as cooked vegetables.

## Method

1. Soak rice noodles until pliable and drain. Mix soy, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sugar and water.
2. Stir-fry the thinly sliced chicken thighs until browned and cooked; transfer it out.
3. Stir-fry broccoli, capsicum, garlic and ginger.
4. Return protein, add noodles and sauce, toss until absorbed, and serve.
