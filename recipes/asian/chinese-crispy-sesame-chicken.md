**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-size
- 1 1/2 tbsp cornflour, for coating
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1/2 tbsp cornflour, for sauce
- 60 ml water
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 each broccoli
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maggie Zhu, “Crispy Sesame Tofu,” [https://omnivorescookbook.com/crispy-sesame-tofu/](https://omnivorescookbook.com/crispy-sesame-tofu/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Zhu's crispy sesame tofu sauce and method as an inspired chicken adaptation.
- Chicken is cut small for a quick cornflour coating; broccoli and rice replace raw garnishes.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Pat the protein dry and coat it with cornflour.
2. Pan-fry the bite-size chicken thighs in oil until crisp and cooked through.
3. Mix soy, vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, cornflour and water; bubble until glossy.
4. Toss through sesame seeds and serve with broccoli and rice.
