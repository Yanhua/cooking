**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 1 each broccoli
- 1 each carrot
- 20 g ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Cook rice in the rice cooker. Slice chicken thinly, broccoli into small florets and carrot into thin half-moons.
2. Heat oil in a wok. Stir-fry chicken for 5–7 minutes until cooked through; transfer to a plate.
3. Stir-fry vegetables with a splash of water for 4–5 minutes. Add minced ginger and garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Return chicken, add soy and oyster sauces, and toss for 1 minute. Serve with rice.
