**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 1 red capsicum
- 50 g roasted peanuts
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1/2 brown onion
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/2 tbsp cornflour
- 45 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maggie Zhu, “Kung Pao Chicken,” [https://omnivorescookbook.com/kung-pao-chicken/](https://omnivorescookbook.com/kung-pao-chicken/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Zhu's kung pao chicken sauce and pan sequence to browned firm tofu.
- Keeps peanuts, capsicum, chilli, vinegar and sweet-savoury sauce; water replaces chicken stock.

## Method

1. Start the rice and mix soy, vinegar, sugar, oyster sauce, cornflour and water.
2. Cook the firm tofu in hot oil until browned; remove it.
3. Stir-fry garlic, ginger, chilli and capsicum until fragrant.
4. Return protein, add peanuts and sauce, toss until bubbling, and serve over rice.
