**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 150 g pork mince
- 300 g firm tofu, cubed
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1/2 brown onion
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1/2 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 150 ml chicken stock
- 1/2 tbsp cornflour
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 2 spring onions
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maggie Zhu, “Tofu With Minced Pork,” [https://omnivorescookbook.com/tofu-with-minced-pork/](https://omnivorescookbook.com/tofu-with-minced-pork/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Builds on Zhu's tofu-with-minced-pork braise and uses Maangchi's mapadubu flavour cue.
- Gochujang and gochugaru replace unavailable doubanjiang, disclosed as an accessible cross-cultural adaptation.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Mix gochujang, gochugaru, soy, stock and cornflour.
2. Cook the pork mince with garlic, ginger and onion until browned.
3. Add sauce and simmer; fold in tofu gently for 3–4 minutes.
4. Stir in sesame oil and spring onion. Serve over rice.
