**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 150 g mushrooms
- 1 each broccoli
- 1 carrot
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 90 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maggie Zhu, “Moo Goo Gai Pan,” [https://omnivorescookbook.com/moo-goo-gai-pan/](https://omnivorescookbook.com/moo-goo-gai-pan/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Zhu's 30-minute homestyle moo goo gai pan and keeps chicken, mushroom, broccoli, carrot and savoury sauce.
- Uses standard boneless thighs and rice; all vegetables are cooked.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Slice chicken, mushrooms, broccoli and carrot.
2. Mix soy, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornflour and water.
3. Stir-fry chicken in oil, then add mushrooms, broccoli, carrot, garlic and ginger until tender-crisp.
4. Add sauce and toss until thickened. Serve with rice.
