**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 300 g cabbage
- 1 each carrot
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start the rice. Thinly shred cabbage and slice carrot.
2. Brown pork in oil for 5–6 minutes, breaking it apart.
3. Add carrot and cabbage; stir-fry for 6–8 minutes until tender. Add minced garlic for 30 seconds.
4. Stir in soy sauce and vinegar. Cook for another minute and serve with rice.
