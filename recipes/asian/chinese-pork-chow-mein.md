**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g thinly sliced pork loin
- 180 g dried egg noodles
- 200 g cabbage, shredded
- 125 g mushrooms
- 1 carrot
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 45 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Maggie Zhu, “Chinese Beef Chow Mein,” [https://omnivorescookbook.com/beef-chow-mein/](https://omnivorescookbook.com/beef-chow-mein/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Zhu's beef chow mein as the foundation with thin pork loin, a deliberate protein swap.
- Adds mushrooms and cabbage; the recipe is sized for two and uses no raw garnish.

## Method

1. Soak or boil noodles until barely tender and drain. Mix soy, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sesame oil and water.
2. Stir-fry the thinly sliced pork loin until cooked, then transfer it out.
3. Stir-fry cabbage, carrot, capsicum, garlic and ginger until tender.
4. Return protein, add noodles and sauce, and toss until steaming.
