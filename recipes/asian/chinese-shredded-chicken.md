**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes including pressure release

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 20 g ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 150 ml water
- 1 bunch baby bok choy
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maggie Zhu, “Chinese-Style Instant Pot Shredded Chicken,” [https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-style-instant-pot-shredded-chicken/](https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-style-instant-pot-shredded-chicken/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Zhu's pressure-cooker meal-prep recipe to two and uses boneless thighs, which the source identifies as forgiving.
- Retains oyster sauce, soy, ginger and garlic; serves with rice and bok choy rather than cold salad.

## Method

1. Put chicken, soy, oyster sauce, ginger, garlic and water in the pressure cooker.
2. Cook on high pressure for 10 minutes, allow 5 minutes natural release, then release the rest.
3. Shred chicken in its liquid and taste for seasoning.
4. Serve with rice and steamed bok choy.
