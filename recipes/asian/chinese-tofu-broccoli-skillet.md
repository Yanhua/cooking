**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 1 each broccoli
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 90 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maggie Zhu, “Chinese Beef and Broccoli,” [https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-beef-and-broccoli/](https://omnivorescookbook.com/chinese-beef-and-broccoli/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Zhu's beef-and-broccoli sauce to firm tofu; tofu is browned first so it survives the saucy finish.
- Use vegetarian oyster-style sauce if required; broccoli and rice remain cooked sides.

## Method

1. Start the rice and stir soy, oyster sauce, Shaoxing wine, sugar, cornflour and water together.
2. Sear the firm tofu in batches until browned; transfer it out.
3. Cook broccoli, garlic and ginger with a splash of water until tender.
4. Return the protein, add sauce, toss until glossy, and serve over rice.
