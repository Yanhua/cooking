**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 300 g green beans
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1 1/2 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Pork Stir Fry with Green Beans,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/stir-fried-green-beans-with-pork/](https://www.recipetineats.com/stir-fried-green-beans-with-pork/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Nagi's green-bean stir-fry to firm tofu, an inspired vegetarian departure; brown tofu separately.
- Keeps charred beans and dark-soy/Shaoxing seasoning and adds rice.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Char green beans in a hot pan with half the oil until blistered; transfer them out.
2. Cook the firm tofu with garlic and ginger in remaining oil until browned and cooked.
3. Add dark soy, Shaoxing wine, sugar and chilli; toss to coat.
4. Return beans and serve over rice.
