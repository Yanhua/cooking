**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu
- 250 g mushrooms
- 1 bunch baby bok choy
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 0.5 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice. Pat tofu dry and cube; slice mushrooms and bok choy.
2. Pan-fry tofu in oil for 6–8 minutes, turning gently.
3. Add mushrooms and cook for 4 minutes. Add minced garlic and ginger, then soy, sugar and 100 ml water.
4. Simmer for 4 minutes. Add bok choy and cook for 3 minutes until tender. Serve with rice.
