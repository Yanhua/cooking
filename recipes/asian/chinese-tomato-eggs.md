**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes, including rice and preparation  
**Total time:** approximately 35 minutes  
**Status:** Not yet cooked

## Ingredients

- 500 g fresh tomatoes, small wedges
- 1 spring onion, finely sliced; all cooked
- 4 eggs
- ½ tsp salt, divided equally
- ¼ tsp black pepper
- ½ tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 3 tbsp cooking oil, divided 2 + 1
- ½ tbsp brown sugar (1½ tsp)
- 150 g dry long-grain white rice
- 360 ml water: 300 ml for rice, 60 ml for sauce

## Source

Sarah Leung, [Chinese Tomato Egg Stir-fry](https://thewoksoflife.com/stir-fried-tomato-and-egg/), The Woks of Life. Website recipe, updated 16 April 2025; accessed 12 September 2026. The page reports re-testing and provides quantities and cooking cues; this household adaptation has only been desk-reviewed.

## Adaptations

- Retains the source's two-serving egg/tomato quantities, separate scrambling, sesame oil and Shaoxing wine.
- Uses catalogued black pepper and brown sugar instead of white pepper and white sugar; expect a different pepper aroma and a slight molasses note. Reduces sugar from 2 to 1½ tsp and salt from ¾ to ½ tsp.
- Adds measured rice and its cooking water for a complete dinner. Allows longer elapsed time than the source's stir-fry-only estimate.
- Uses a wide frying pan with a lid; cooks every spring-onion piece. Fresh tomato pack size of 500 g is an editable planning estimate, not a verified supermarket pack. Buy loose where practical. Shaoxing is retained for its aroma; dry sherry is the source's suggested alternative, with a different flavour.

## Method

1. Put rice and 300 ml water in a small saucepan. Bring to a boil, cover, reduce to low and cook for 12–15 minutes until absorbed; rest covered off heat for 10 minutes. Check the rice packet and adjust the measured water if its instructions differ.
2. Meanwhile, prepare tomatoes and spring onion. Beat eggs with ¼ tsp salt, pepper, sesame oil and wine.
3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wide frying pan over medium-high heat. Scramble eggs into soft curds, then transfer to a bowl.
4. Add remaining oil, tomatoes and all spring onion. Stir for 1 minute. Add sugar, remaining salt and 60 ml water; cover and simmer for 2–4 minutes until tomatoes and onion soften.
5. Fold eggs back in. Cook uncovered until eggs are fully set and sauce lightly coats them; leave enough juices for the rice. Divide rice and stir-fry between two bowls.
