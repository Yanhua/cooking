**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g beef rump steak, thinly sliced
- 1 red capsicum, thinly sliced
- 150 g canned bamboo shoots, drained
- 1 1/2 tbsp cooking oil
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 45 ml water
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Chinjao Rosu (Japanese Stir Fry with Beef and Bamboo Shoots),” [https://japan.recipetineats.com/japanese-stir-fry-with-beef-and-bamboo-shoots/](https://japan.recipetineats.com/japanese-stir-fry-with-beef-and-bamboo-shoots/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales RecipeTin Japan's recipe to two and retains its cornflour marinade, capsicum, bamboo shoots and soy-oyster sauce.
- The preferred protein is thin rump steak; mince is not substituted because the source depends on sliced strips.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Mix the protein with soy, sake and cornflour; mix the sauce separately.
2. Stir-fry capsicum and bamboo shoots until tender, then transfer out.
3. Stir-fry the thinly sliced rump steak in remaining oil until browned and nearly cooked.
4. Return vegetables, add sauce, toss until thickened, and serve with rice.
