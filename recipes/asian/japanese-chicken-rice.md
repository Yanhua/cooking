**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 1 each broccoli
- 1 each carrot
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice. Cut chicken into small pieces and vegetables into bite-sized pieces.
2. Steam broccoli and carrot until tender, about 6–8 minutes.
3. Fry chicken in oil for 6–8 minutes until cooked through.
4. Add soy, honey, vinegar and 2 tbsp water. Simmer for 2 minutes to glaze. Serve with vegetables and rice.
