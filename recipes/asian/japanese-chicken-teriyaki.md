**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, boneless and skinless
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 each broccoli, cut into florets
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Chicken Teriyaki,” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scaled Chen's pan-seared two-person technique to 350 g chicken thighs.
- Uses cooked broccoli and rice instead of raw accompaniments; mirin and sake retain the source's tare balance.

## Method

1. Start the rice in the rice cooker.
2. Pat the boneless chicken thighs dry and cut it into even pieces. Mix the soy sauce, mirin, sake and sugar.
3. Sear the boneless chicken thighs in oil until browned and cooked through.
4. Pour in the sauce and simmer until glossy. Serve with the cooked vegetables and rice.
