**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 200 g firm tofu
- 300 g cabbage, shredded
- 150 g mushrooms
- 15 g ginger
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 500 ml water
- 2 spring onions
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Hot Pot for One,” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/hot-pot-for-one/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/hot-pot-for-one/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Chen's listed chicken option for the hot-pot format and scales the one-person recipe to two.
- Keeps the tofu, cabbage, mushrooms and gingery broth; chicken is sliced thinly for the source's 5–8 minute cooking window.

## Method

1. Start rice. Bring water, ginger, soy and sesame oil to a simmer in a wide saucepan.
2. Add cabbage and mushrooms and cook 2 minutes.
3. Arrange the boneless chicken thighs and tofu over the vegetables, cover and simmer until cooked through.
4. Taste broth, add spring onion, and serve with rice.
