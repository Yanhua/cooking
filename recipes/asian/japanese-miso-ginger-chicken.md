**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 25 g ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 30 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 each broccoli
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Miso Ginger Pork,” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/miso-ginger-pork/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/miso-ginger-pork/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Chen's miso ginger pork seasoning to boneless chicken thighs.
- Keeps the short ginger marinade and pan finish; broccoli is cooked in the same pan and rice replaces raw accompaniments.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Mix ginger, sake and soy sauce, coat the protein and rest briefly.
2. Sear the boneless chicken thighs in oil until just cooked through.
3. Whisk miso, soy, sake, mirin, sugar and water; pour into the pan and turn the protein until thickened.
4. Serve with cooked vegetables and rice.
