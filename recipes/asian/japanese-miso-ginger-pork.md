**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes including a short rest

## Ingredients

- 350 g pork loin, thinly sliced
- 25 g ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 30 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 250 g cabbage, cooked
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Miso Ginger Pork,” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/miso-ginger-pork/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/miso-ginger-pork/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Chen's two-serving thin-pork recipe and preserves the ginger, miso, soy, sake and mirin structure.
- Uses cooked cabbage and rice in place of raw shredded cabbage.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Mix ginger, sake and soy sauce, coat the protein and rest briefly.
2. Sear the pork loin in oil until just cooked through.
3. Whisk miso, soy, sake, mirin, sugar and water; pour into the pan and turn the protein until thickened.
4. Serve with cooked vegetables and rice.
