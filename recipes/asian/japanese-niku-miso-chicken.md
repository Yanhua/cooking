**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into 2 cm pieces
- 15 g ginger, grated
- 2 spring onions
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 60 ml water
- 250 g cabbage, shredded and cooked
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Easy Niku Miso (Miso Meat Sauce),” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/10-minute-meal-niku-miso/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/10-minute-meal-niku-miso/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Chen's explicitly suggested chicken substitution in the niku miso sauce.
- Adds cooked cabbage and rice; the sauce is held back slightly because chicken releases less fat than pork mince.

## Method

1. Start the rice and cook the vegetables until tender.
2. Stir miso, soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, cornflour and water until smooth.
3. Cook the boneless chicken thighs with ginger and spring onion in sesame oil until browned and cooked through.
4. Add sauce and simmer until it clings. Serve over rice.
