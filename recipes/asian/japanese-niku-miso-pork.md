**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 15 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g pork mince
- 15 g ginger, grated
- 2 spring onions, thinly sliced
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp white miso
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 60 ml water
- 1 zucchini, sliced and cooked
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Easy Niku Miso (Miso Meat Sauce),” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/10-minute-meal-niku-miso/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/10-minute-meal-niku-miso/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps Chen's 15-minute miso meat sauce and scales it to 300 g pork mince.
- Serves it over rice with cooked zucchini; no raw lettuce cups are used.

## Method

1. Start the rice and cook the vegetables until tender.
2. Stir miso, soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar, cornflour and water until smooth.
3. Cook the pork mince with ginger and spring onion in sesame oil until browned and cooked through.
4. Add sauce and simmer until it clings. Serve over rice.
