**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 250 g cabbage, finely shredded and squeezed dry
- 75 g mushrooms, finely chopped
- 2 spring onions
- 10 g ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 20 gyoza wrappers
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 100 ml water
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 bunch baby bok choy

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Gyoza,” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/gyoza/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/gyoza/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Chen's gyoza filling and pan-steam technique but buys wrappers and makes 20 rather than folding the full 52-piece batch.
- Pre-squeezed cabbage keeps active work within 30 minutes; serves with cooked vegetables and soy-vinegar dip.

## Method

1. Mix pork, cabbage, mushrooms, spring onion, ginger, garlic, soy and sesame oil.
2. Place about 1 tbsp filling in each wrapper, fold and press closed; make about 20.
3. Pan-fry dumplings in oil until bottoms are golden, add water, cover and steam 5–6 minutes.
4. Uncover and cook until dry; serve with soy and vinegar.
