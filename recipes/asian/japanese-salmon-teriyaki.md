**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g salmon fillets, skinless
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 bunch baby bok choy
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Chicken Teriyaki,” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Chen's pan-seared teriyaki method for salmon, a variation linked from the source page.
- Scales the sauce to two fillets and serves cooked bok choy; fish is removed as soon as it flakes.

## Method

1. Start the rice in the rice cooker.
2. Pat the salmon fillets dry and cut it into even pieces. Mix the soy sauce, mirin, sake and sugar.
3. Sear the salmon fillets in oil until browned and cooked through.
4. Pour in the sauce and simmer until glossy. Serve with the cooked vegetables and rice.
