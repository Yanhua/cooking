**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and slabbed
- 1 tbsp cornflour, for coating
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tbsp cooking sake
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Namiko Hirasawa Chen, “Chicken Teriyaki,” [https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/](https://www.justonecookbook.com/chicken-teriyaki/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Chen's pan-glazed teriyaki method to firm tofu, a variation linked from the source page.
- Presses and cornflour-coats tofu for browning; the sauce keeps the soy–mirin–sake–sugar balance.

## Method

1. Start the rice in the rice cooker.
2. Pat the firm tofu dry and cut it into even pieces. Mix the soy sauce, mirin, sake and sugar.
3. Sear the firm tofu in oil until browned and cooked through.
4. Pour in the sauce and simmer until glossy. Serve with the cooked vegetables and rice.
