**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 55 minutes including marinade

## Ingredients

- 300 g beef rump steak, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic
- 10 g ginger
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 brown onion
- 125 g mushrooms
- 1 bunch baby bok choy
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Easy Bulgogi (Mak-bulgogi),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/easy-bulgogi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/easy-bulgogi) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Maangchi's two-serving beef BBQ to a quick skillet meal and retains the soy, sesame oil, sugar and garlic marinade.
- Omits raw lettuce and green-onion salad, cooking mushrooms and onion instead and serving with rice.

## Method

1. Mix soy, sugar, sesame oil, ginger, garlic and pepper; coat the protein and rest 20–30 minutes.
2. Cook the beef rump steak in batches in a very hot pan so the edges caramelise.
3. Add onion and mushrooms and cook until tender.
4. Serve with rice and blanched bok choy; these replace raw wrap accompaniments.
