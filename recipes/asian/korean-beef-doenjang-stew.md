**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 150 g beef rump steak, thinly sliced
- 150 g firm tofu
- 200 g potatoes, diced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp doenjang
- 1/2 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 450 ml water
- 2 spring onions
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Soybean Paste Stew with Beef (Sogogi Doenjang-jjigae),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/sogogi-doenjang-jjigae](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/sogogi-doenjang-jjigae) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Maangchi's soybean-paste stew and keeps the beef, potato, zucchini, tofu, garlic and doenjang.
- Uses a small quantity of thin steak and ready-made water-based broth; vegetables are fully cooked.

## Method

1. Start rice. Heat oil and cook the beef rump steak with garlic until browned.
2. Add water, doenjang and gochugaru and bring to a simmer.
3. Add potato, onion, zucchini and tofu; cover and cook about 12 minutes.
4. Taste, add spring onion, and serve with rice.
