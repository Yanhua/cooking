**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200 g beef rump steak, finely chopped
- 300 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 spring onions
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 1/2 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 450 ml water
- 2 eggs
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Spicy Soft Tofu Stew with Beef (Gogi Sundubu-jjigae),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/gogi-sundubu-jjigae](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/gogi-sundubu-jjigae) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Maangchi's beef sundubu-jjigae to two and uses firm tofu for local availability rather than soft tofu tubes.
- Uses ready-made stock and cooked onion; the eggs are cooked directly in the simmering stew.

## Method

1. Mix gochugaru, sesame oil and pepper. Start rice.
2. Cook the beef rump steak with onion and garlic in oil until no longer pink.
3. Add water, fish sauce and the spicy seasoning; simmer 5 minutes.
4. Add tofu, cook 3–4 minutes, crack in eggs and cover until set. Serve with rice.
