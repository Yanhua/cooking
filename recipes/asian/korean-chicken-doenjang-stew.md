**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-size
- 150 g firm tofu
- 200 g potatoes, diced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp doenjang
- 1/2 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 450 ml water
- 2 spring onions
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Soybean Paste Stew with Beef (Sogogi Doenjang-jjigae),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/sogogi-doenjang-jjigae](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/sogogi-doenjang-jjigae) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Applies Maangchi's doenjang-jjigae vegetables and seasoning to chicken thighs, a deliberate protein adaptation.
- Uses cooked zucchini, potato, onion and tofu; the stew is served with rice and no raw side.

## Method

1. Start rice. Heat oil and cook the boneless chicken thighs with garlic until browned.
2. Add water, doenjang and gochugaru and bring to a simmer.
3. Add potato, onion, zucchini and tofu; cover and cook about 12 minutes.
4. Taste, add spring onion, and serve with rice.
