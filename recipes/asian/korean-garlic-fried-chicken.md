**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, small pieces
- 10 g ginger
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 3 tbsp cornflour
- 3 tbsp cooking oil
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 2 spring onions
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 45 ml water
- 1 each broccoli
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Spicy Garlic Fried Chicken (Kkanpunggi),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/kkanpunggi/comment-page-1](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/kkanpunggi/comment-page-1) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Maangchi's boneless kkanpunggi and uses shallow frying to keep active work and oil volume practical.
- Keeps the crisp cornflour coating, garlic-chilli oil and sweet-sour soy glaze; rice and cooked broccoli replace raw accompaniments.

## Method

1. Toss chicken with ginger, soy, pepper and cornflour.
2. Shallow-fry chicken in batches until crisp, golden and cooked through.
3. Stir-fry garlic, chilli and spring onion in the same pan.
4. Add soy, vinegar, sugar and water; bubble briefly, return chicken, toss, and serve with rice.
