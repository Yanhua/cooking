**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-size
- 1 1/2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 45 ml water
- 2 cloves garlic
- 10 g ginger
- 1 red capsicum
- 1 each broccoli
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Spicy Korean Pork Stir Fry,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/spicy-korean-pork-stir-fry/](https://www.recipetineats.com/spicy-korean-pork-stir-fry/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses the marinade from Nagi's spicy Korean pork stir-fry as a quick sauce for chicken thighs.
- Adds cooked capsicum and broccoli, shortens the marinade to a rest while rice cooks, and uses no raw salad.

## Method

1. Start rice. Stir gochujang, soy, honey, sesame oil and water together.
2. Cook the boneless chicken thighs in a hot pan until browned and cooked through.
3. Add garlic, ginger, capsicum and broccoli and stir-fry until tender.
4. Add sauce and toss until sticky; serve over rice with sesame seeds.
