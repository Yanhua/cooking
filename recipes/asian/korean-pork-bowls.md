**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 300 g cabbage
- 1 each carrot
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 0.5 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice. Shred cabbage and slice carrot finely.
2. Brown pork in oil for 5–6 minutes.
3. Add vegetables and stir-fry for 6–8 minutes until tender.
4. Stir in gochujang, soy, sugar and 2 tbsp water. Cook for 2 minutes; finish with sesame oil and serve with rice.
