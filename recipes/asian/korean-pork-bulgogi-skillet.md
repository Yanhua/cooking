**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 55 minutes including marinade

## Ingredients

- 300 g pork loin, thinly sliced
- 1 1/2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 3 cloves garlic
- 10 g ginger
- 1/2 brown onion
- 125 g mushrooms
- 1 bunch baby bok choy
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Dwaeji Bulgogi (Spicy Pork Bulgogi BBQ),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/dwaeji-bulgogi](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/dwaeji-bulgogi) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Maangchi's spicy pork bulgogi marinade with pork loin instead of belly, a leaner and faster adaptation.
- Keeps gochujang, soy, sugar, garlic, ginger and sesame oil; mushrooms and onion are cooked sides.

## Method

1. Mix soy, sugar, sesame oil, ginger, garlic and pepper; coat the protein and rest 20–30 minutes.
2. Cook the pork loin in batches in a very hot pan so the edges caramelise.
3. Add onion and mushrooms and cook until tender.
4. Serve with rice and blanched bok choy; these replace raw wrap accompaniments.
