**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200 g pork mince
- 300 g firm tofu, cubed
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 spring onions
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 1/2 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 450 ml water
- 2 eggs
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Spicy Soft Tofu Stew with Beef (Gogi Sundubu-jjigae),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/gogi-sundubu-jjigae](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/gogi-sundubu-jjigae) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Maangchi's beef sundubu method with pork mince, a disclosed protein adaptation that cooks quickly.
- Firm tofu and ready-made stock replace soft tofu tubes and homemade anchovy-kelp stock; eggs are poached in the stew.

## Method

1. Mix gochugaru, sesame oil and pepper. Start rice.
2. Cook the pork mince with onion and garlic in oil until no longer pink.
3. Add water, fish sauce and the spicy seasoning; simmer 5 minutes.
4. Add tofu, cook 3–4 minutes, crack in eggs and cover until set. Serve with rice.
