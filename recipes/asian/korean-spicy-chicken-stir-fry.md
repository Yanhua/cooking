**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes including marinade

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 10 g ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 brown onion
- 250 g cabbage
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Spicy Korean Pork Stir Fry,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/spicy-korean-pork-stir-fry/](https://www.recipetineats.com/spicy-korean-pork-stir-fry/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Applies Nagi's explicitly flexible spicy Korean marinade to chicken thighs.
- Cuts the marinade rest to 20 minutes and cooks the onion and cabbage; rice replaces raw salad.

## Method

1. Mix gochujang, gochugaru, soy, sugar, sesame oil, ginger and garlic; coat the protein and rest 20 minutes.
2. Cook the boneless chicken thighs with onion in a very hot pan until deeply coloured and cooked through.
3. Add cabbage and a splash of water and toss until softened and sticky.
4. Serve with rice and cooked vegetables.
