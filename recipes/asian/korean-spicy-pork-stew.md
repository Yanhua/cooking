**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 1/2 brown onion
- 300 g potatoes, diced
- 150 g firm tofu, cubed
- 2 cloves garlic
- 10 g ginger
- 1 1/2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 500 ml water
- 2 spring onions
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Spicy Pork Stew (Dwaejigogi-jjigae),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/dwaejigogi-jjigae](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/dwaejigogi-jjigae) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Maangchi's 25-minute dwaejigogi-jjigae to two and adapts sliced pork belly to pork mince.
- Keeps gochujang, gochugaru, sesame oil, potato, tofu and fish sauce; all vegetables are simmered.

## Method

1. Mix gochujang, gochugaru, sesame oil, ginger, garlic and pepper.
2. Put onion, potato and pork mince in a saucepan and toss with the paste.
3. Add water and fish sauce, cover and simmer 20–25 minutes until tender.
4. Fold in tofu and spring onion for the final 3 minutes; serve with rice.
