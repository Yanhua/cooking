**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, bite-size
- 2 tbsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp tomato ketchup
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 each broccoli
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Korean-style Mapo Tofu (Mapadubu),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/korean-style-mapo-tofu](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/korean-style-mapo-tofu) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Maangchi's dubu-gangjeong sauce to two and keeps the sweet, sour, spicy gochujang coating.
- Uses pan-frying rather than a deep pot of oil, adds cooked broccoli and rice, and leaves out garlic/onion as in the temple-cuisine source.

## Method

1. Start rice. Pat tofu dry and coat it with cornflour.
2. Pan-fry tofu in oil until golden and crisp.
3. Simmer gochujang, ketchup, honey and vinegar for 1–2 minutes.
4. Turn tofu through the sauce and serve with broccoli and rice.
