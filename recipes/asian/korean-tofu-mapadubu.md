**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, cubed
- 120 g pork mince
- 2 cloves garlic
- 10 g ginger
- 1/2 brown onion
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1/2 tsp Korean chilli flakes
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 150 ml chicken stock
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 2 spring onions
- 1/2 tbsp cornflour
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Maangchi, “Korean-style Mapo Tofu (Mapadubu),” [https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/korean-style-mapo-tofu](https://www.maangchi.com/recipe/korean-style-mapo-tofu) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Maangchi's 2–4 serving mapadubu to two and keeps tofu, garlic, ginger, gochujang, onion and sesame oil.
- Uses firm supermarket tofu and cooked zucchini instead of the source's green chilli and stock; rice is the only side needed.

## Method

1. Start rice. Stir gochujang, gochugaru, soy, stock and cornflour together.
2. Cook the firm tofu with garlic, ginger and onion until browned.
3. Add sauce and simmer; fold in tofu gently for 3–4 minutes.
4. Stir in sesame oil and spring onion and serve over rice.
