**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 2 tsp curry powder
- 1 each carrot
- 200 g green beans
- 0.5 each brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice. Slice chicken, onion and carrot; trim beans.
2. Fry onion and chicken in oil for 5 minutes. Add minced garlic and curry powder for 30 seconds.
3. Add coconut milk and carrot. Simmer for 8 minutes.
4. Add beans and simmer for 5 minutes until chicken is cooked through and vegetables are tender. Serve with rice.
