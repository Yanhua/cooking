**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes including covered cooking

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-size
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1/2 brown onion, diced
- 1 red capsicum, diced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 200 g canned tomatoes
- 300 ml chicken stock
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Nando's Portuguese Chicken and Rice,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/portuguese-chicken-and-rice-one-pot-recipe/](https://www.recipetineats.com/portuguese-chicken-and-rice-one-pot-recipe/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Nagi's one-pot Portuguese chicken and rice to two and keeps the peri-peri-style paprika, cumin and chilli seasoning.
- Uses the oven-safe stovetop pan method with cooked onion and capsicum; the sauce is omitted to keep the card within the catalogue.

## Method

1. Mix paprika, cumin, chilli, garlic, salt and oil and coat the chicken.
2. Sear chicken in a wide lidded pan until browned; remove it.
3. Cook rice with onion, capsicum, tomato paste and stock. Nestle chicken on top, cover and simmer 15 minutes.
4. Rest covered 5 minutes, fluff, and serve.
