**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 180 g dried rice noodles
- 2 eggs
- 1/2 brown onion, sliced
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 100 g frozen peas
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 2 tsp Shaoxing cooking wine
- 2 1/2 tsp curry powder
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1 1/2 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Singapore Noodles,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/singapore-noodles/](https://www.recipetineats.com/singapore-noodles/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Nagi's two-serving Singapore noodle method with chicken in place of the source's char siu and prawns.
- Retains curry powder, soy, Shaoxing wine, egg and vegetables; onion and capsicum are cooked.

## Method

1. Soak rice noodles until pliable and drain. Mix soy, Shaoxing wine, curry powder, sugar and pepper.
2. Scramble the eggs in half the oil and transfer out.
3. Stir-fry the boneless chicken thighs until cooked, then add onion, capsicum and garlic.
4. Add noodles, peas and sauce; toss until hot and fold through egg.
