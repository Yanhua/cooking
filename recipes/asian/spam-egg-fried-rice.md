# Spam egg fried rice

**Serves:** 4–6
**Active time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice from 2 1/2 cups uncooked rice, ideally chilled overnight
- 4 eggs
- 200–300 g Spam, diced into small pieces
- 3–4 spring onions, sliced, with the white and green parts kept separate
- 1 cup frozen peas or mixed vegetables
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp kecap manis
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil, optional
- White or black pepper, to taste
- Neutral cooking oil

## Method

1. Break up any clumps in the chilled rice with your hands.
2. Heat a wok or large frying pan over high heat. Add 1 tbsp oil, then scramble the eggs until about 80% cooked. Transfer to a plate.
3. Add the Spam to the dry pan and fry until browned and lightly crisp at the edges. If it releases a lot of fat, carefully remove any excess.
4. Add the spring-onion whites and frozen peas. Stir-fry for 1–2 minutes.
5. Increase the heat to high, add the rice, and stir-fry vigorously for 3–5 minutes until hot and the grains are separate.
6. Add the soy sauce, kecap manis, and a few grinds of pepper. Toss well.
7. Return the eggs, add the spring-onion greens and, if using, sesame oil. Stir-fry for another 30 seconds, then serve.

## Tips

- Avoid overcrowding the pan; cook in two batches if needed.
- Keep the heat high and the sauce light so the rice stays fluffy rather than wet.
- For a richer finish, use 5 eggs and stir one reserved yolk through the hot rice just before serving.
