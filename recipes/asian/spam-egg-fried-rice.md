**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 180 g dry rice
- 2 each eggs
- 150 g Spam
- 2 each spring onions
- 100 g frozen peas
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 0.5 tbsp kecap manis
- 0.25 tsp sesame oil
- 0.25 tsp black pepper
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Method

1. Dice the Spam, slice spring onions and keep the white and green parts separate.
2. Use cooked rice made from 180 g dry rice, prepared ahead and chilled. Break up any clumps.
3. Heat a wok or large frying pan over high heat. Add 1 tbsp oil, then scramble the eggs until about 80% cooked. Transfer to a plate.
4. Add the Spam to the dry pan and fry until browned and lightly crisp at the edges. If it releases a lot of fat, carefully remove any excess.
5. Add the spring-onion whites and frozen peas. Stir-fry for 1–2 minutes.
6. Increase the heat to high, add the rice, and stir-fry vigorously for 3–5 minutes until hot and the grains are separate.
7. Add the soy sauce, kecap manis, and a few grinds of pepper. Toss well.
8. Return the eggs, add the spring-onion greens and, if using, sesame oil. Stir-fry for another 2 minutes until the greens are cooked, then serve.
