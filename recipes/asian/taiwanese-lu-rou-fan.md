**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes; pressure cooking adds hands-off time

## Ingredients

- 300 g pork mince
- 1 each brown onion
- 125 g mushrooms
- 3 cloves garlic
- 4 each eggs
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp Chinese five-spice
- 1 each star anise
- 250 ml water
- 1 bunch baby bok choy
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Finely chop onion, mushrooms and garlic; halve bok choy lengthways.
2. Start the measured dry rice in the rice cooker according to its instructions.
3. Cook the eggs in the pressure cooker on high pressure for 5 minutes, then release pressure and cool them under cold water. Peel and set aside. (Alternatively, hard-boil them on the stovetop.)
4. Using the sauté function, cook pork, onion, mushrooms, and garlic for 6–8 minutes, breaking up the pork until browned. Drain excess fat if needed.
5. Stir in both soy sauces, sugar, vinegar, five-spice, star anise, and water. Nestle in the peeled eggs. Pressure-cook on high for 18 minutes, then quick-release.
6. While it cooks, steam or blanch bok choy until just tender and cook rice in the rice cooker. Remove star anise, halve the eggs, and spoon the pork and sauce over rice with bok choy.
