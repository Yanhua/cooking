**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 200 g green beans
- 1 each red capsicum
- 2 cloves garlic
- 30 g Thai basil
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1.5 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 0.33 tbsp fish sauce
- 0.33 tbsp brown sugar
- 0.5 tsp chilli flakes
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Thinly slice chicken and capsicum. Trim and halve beans and mince garlic.
2. Start the measured dry rice in the rice cooker according to its instructions.
3. Mix oyster sauce, soy sauce, fish sauce, sugar, and chilli (if using) with 2 tbsp water.
4. Heat a large frying pan or wok until very hot. Add oil, then chicken; stir-fry for 4–5 minutes until browned and cooked through.
5. Add garlic, beans, and capsicum. Stir-fry for 3–4 minutes, keeping the vegetables bright and tender-crisp.
6. Add the sauce and toss for about 1 minute until it lightly coats the chicken. Turn off the heat, fold through basil, and serve with rice.
