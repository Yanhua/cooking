**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 200 g dried egg noodles
- 1 each carrot
- 1 each red capsicum
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 0.5 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp chilli flakes

## Method

1. Cook noodles following the packet and drain.
2. Thinly slice chicken and vegetables. Stir-fry chicken in oil for 5–7 minutes until cooked through.
3. Add vegetables and cook for 4 minutes; add minced garlic.
4. Toss in noodles, sauces, sugar and chilli with a splash of water. Cook for 2 minutes.
