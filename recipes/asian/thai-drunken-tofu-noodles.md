**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 220 g dried rice noodles
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 red capsicum
- 1 each broccoli
- 30 g Thai basil
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 45 ml water
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Pailin Chongchitnant, “Authentic Drunken Noodles (Pad Kee Mao),” [https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-kee-mao-2/](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-kee-mao-2/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Pailin's pad kee mao to browned firm tofu and keeps the holy-basil, chilli and savoury sauce profile.
- Uses dried rice noodles and cooked broccoli/capsicum; oyster sauce can be replaced with vegetarian stir-fry sauce.

## Method

1. Soak rice noodles until pliable and drain. Mix oyster sauce, soy, fish sauce, sugar, vinegar and water.
2. Stir-fry the firm tofu until cooked and transfer it out.
3. Stir-fry garlic, chilli, capsicum and broccoli until fragrant.
4. Add noodles and sauce, toss until glossy, return protein, and fold through basil.
