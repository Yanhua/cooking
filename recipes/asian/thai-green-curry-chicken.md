**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-size
- 2 tbsp Thai green curry paste
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 120 ml water
- 1 eggplant, cubed
- 1 red capsicum
- 150 g green beans
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 20 g Thai basil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Pailin Chongchitnant, “Authentic & Easy Thai Green Curry Chicken,” [https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/green-curry-new-2/](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/green-curry-new-2/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Pailin's 4-serving green curry to two while keeping the reduced-coconut-milk method and chicken thigh.
- Uses supermarket eggplant, capsicum and beans; the source's green curry paste is catalogued separately from red paste.

## Method

1. Start rice. Reduce half the coconut milk in a saucepan, then fry green curry paste in it for 1 minute.
2. Add the boneless chicken thighs and coat it; pour in remaining coconut milk and water.
3. Add eggplant, capsicum and beans and simmer until tender.
4. Season with fish sauce and sugar, fold through basil, and serve over rice.
