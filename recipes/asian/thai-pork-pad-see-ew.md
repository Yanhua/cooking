**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 225 g pork loin, thinly sliced
- 250 g dried rice noodles
- 1 each broccoli
- 2 eggs
- 4 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 2 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1/2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Pailin Chongchitnant, “Authentic Thai Pad See Ew,” [https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-see-ew-new/](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-see-ew-new/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Pailin's source-supported pork option for pad see ew and scales it to two.
- Dried rice noodles replace fresh wide noodles; broccoli is cooked as the gai-lan substitute.

## Method

1. Soak rice noodles until pliable and drain. Mix oyster sauce, soy, fish sauce, dark soy and sugar.
2. Stir-fry the pork loin until cooked and transfer it out.
3. Add oil, garlic and egg; scramble, then add broccoli and cook until tender-crisp.
4. Add noodles and sauce and toss over high heat; return protein and serve.
