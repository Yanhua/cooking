# Thai red curry pork with carrots and capsicum

**Serves:** 2  
**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 1 can (400 ml) coconut milk
- 1 1/2–2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 2 carrots, thinly sliced
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- Steamed rice, to serve

## Method

1. Heat oil in a wide pan. Cook pork mince for 4–5 minutes, breaking it up until browned.
2. Add garlic and curry paste; fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
3. Add coconut milk, carrots, capsicum, sugar, and fish sauce. Simmer for 10–12 minutes until the carrots are tender and the sauce has thickened slightly.
4. Taste and adjust with more fish sauce or sugar if needed. Serve with rice.
