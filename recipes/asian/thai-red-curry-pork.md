**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 2 each carrot
- 1 each red capsicum
- 2 cloves garlic
- 0.33 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Thinly slice carrots and capsicum and mince garlic.
2. Start the measured dry rice in the rice cooker according to its instructions.
3. Heat oil in a wide pan. Cook pork mince for 4–5 minutes, breaking it up until browned.
4. Add garlic and curry paste; fry for 1 minute until fragrant.
5. Add coconut milk, carrots, capsicum, sugar, and fish sauce. Simmer for 10–12 minutes until the carrots are tender and the sauce has thickened slightly.
6. Taste and adjust with more fish sauce or sugar if needed. Serve with rice.
