**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 220 g dried rice noodles
- 2 eggs
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 each broccoli
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp dark soy sauce
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 60 ml water
- 1 1/2 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Pailin Chongchitnant, “Rice Noodles with Chicken (Guay Tiew Kua Gai),” [https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/rice-noodles-chicken-guay-tiew-kua-gai/](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/rice-noodles-chicken-guay-tiew-kua-gai/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Pailin's chicken street-food noodle recipe to two and uses dried rice noodles with her soaking method.
- Uses broccoli for the source's leafy Chinese broccoli and serves all vegetables cooked.

## Method

1. Soak rice noodles until pliable and drain. Mix soy, dark soy, sugar, oyster sauce and water.
2. Sear chicken in a hot wok until browned and almost cooked; transfer it out.
3. Scramble egg with garlic, add noodles and sauce, and toss until absorbed.
4. Return chicken, add broccoli, toss until tender, and serve hot.
