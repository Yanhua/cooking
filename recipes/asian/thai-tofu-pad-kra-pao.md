**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 red capsicum
- 1/4 brown onion
- 30 g Thai basil
- 1 tbsp oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 30 ml water
- 1 1/2 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Pailin Chongchitnant, “The Only Pad Kra Pao Recipe You Need,” [https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-kra-pao-anything/](https://hot-thai-kitchen.com/pad-kra-pao-anything/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Pailin's explicit tofu option for pad kra pao and replaces fish sauce with extra soy for a vegetarian direction.
- Browns firm tofu before the sauce, cooks the vegetables, and serves with rice and optional fried eggs.

## Method

1. Start rice and mix oyster sauce, soy, fish sauce, dark soy, sugar and water.
2. Stir-fry garlic and chilli in hot oil, then add the firm tofu and cook until browned.
3. Add onion and capsicum and toss until tender; add sauce and reduce briefly.
4. Turn off heat, fold through basil until wilted, and serve with rice and fried eggs if using.
