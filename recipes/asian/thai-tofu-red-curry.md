**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu
- 1 each broccoli
- 1 each carrot
- 400 ml coconut milk
- 2 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice and cut tofu into cubes and vegetables into small pieces.
2. Fry curry paste in oil for 1 minute.
3. Add coconut milk, carrot and tofu. Simmer for 8 minutes.
4. Add broccoli and soy sauce; simmer for 5 minutes until vegetables are tender. Serve with rice. Choose a vegetarian curry paste if needed.
