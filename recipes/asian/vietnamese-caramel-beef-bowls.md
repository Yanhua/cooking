**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g beef mince
- 1/2 brown onion, diced
- 15 g ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 1/2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 60 ml water
- 200 g green beans
- 1 carrot
- 150 g dry rice
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Vietnamese Caramelised Pork Bowls,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/vietnamese-caramelised-pork-bowls/](https://www.recipetineats.com/vietnamese-caramelised-pork-bowls/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Nagi's explicit beef option for Vietnamese caramelised mince, scaled to two.
- Keeps the caramel, fish sauce, ginger and garlic balance and cooks beans and carrot as the vegetable side.

## Method

1. Start rice. Cook onion, ginger and garlic in oil until fragrant.
2. Sprinkle in sugar and let it melt to amber. Add the beef mince and stir until browned.
3. Add fish sauce, soy and water and simmer until glossy and cooked through.
4. Add beans and carrot for the final few minutes and serve over rice.
