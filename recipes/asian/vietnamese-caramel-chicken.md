**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 200 g green beans
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1.5 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp light soy sauce
- 80 ml water
- 0.25 tsp black pepper
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces, trim beans and mince garlic.
2. Start the measured dry rice in the rice cooker according to its instructions.
3. Mix fish sauce, soy sauce, water, and plenty of black pepper. Heat oil in a wide frying pan and cook chicken for 5–6 minutes until browned.
4. Push chicken aside. Add brown sugar to the bare part of the pan; let it melt and turn deep amber for 30–60 seconds, then carefully stir in the sauce mixture.
5. Add garlic and green beans. Toss and simmer for 5–6 minutes until the chicken is cooked, beans are tender-crisp, and the sauce coats everything. Serve with rice.
