# Vietnamese caramel chicken with green beans

**Serves:** 2  
**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-sized pieces
- 200 g green beans, trimmed
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp neutral oil
- 1 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 80 ml water
- Black pepper
- Steamed rice, to serve

## Method

1. Mix fish sauce, soy sauce, water, and plenty of black pepper. Heat oil in a wide frying pan and cook chicken for 5–6 minutes until browned.
2. Push chicken aside. Add brown sugar to the bare part of the pan; let it melt and turn deep amber for 30–60 seconds, then carefully stir in the sauce mixture.
3. Add garlic and green beans. Toss and simmer for 5–6 minutes until the chicken is cooked, beans are tender-crisp, and the sauce coats everything. Serve with rice.
