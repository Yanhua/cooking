**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 1 tbsp cornflour
- 1/2 brown onion
- 15 g ginger
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 1/2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1/2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 1/2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 60 ml water
- 200 g green beans
- 1 carrot
- 150 g dry rice
- 1 1/2 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Nagi, “Vietnamese Caramelised Pork Bowls,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/vietnamese-caramelised-pork-bowls/](https://www.recipetineats.com/vietnamese-caramelised-pork-bowls/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Nagi's caramelised mince technique to firm tofu, a substantial inspired departure disclosed here.
- Uses soy in place of fish sauce, adds a little cornflour for tofu browning, and keeps cooked beans, carrot and rice.

## Method

1. Start rice. Cook onion, ginger and garlic in oil until fragrant.
2. Sprinkle in sugar and let it melt to amber. Add the firm tofu and stir until browned.
3. Add fish sauce, soy and water and simmer until glossy and cooked through.
4. Add beans and carrot for the final few minutes and serve over rice.
