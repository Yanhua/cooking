**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu
- 200 g green beans
- 2 cloves garlic
- 2 tbsp light soy sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice. Pat tofu dry, cube it and trim beans.
2. Pan-fry tofu in oil for 8 minutes, turning until golden.
3. Add beans with 3 tbsp water, cover and cook for 5 minutes.
4. Add minced garlic, soy, sugar and vinegar. Toss and simmer for 3 minutes until beans are tender and tofu is coated; serve with rice.
