**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 450 g potatoes
- 250 g mushrooms
- 2 cloves garlic
- 80 g grated cheese
- 1 tsp dried thyme and Italian herbs
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Katy Greenwood, “Baked eggs with potatoes, mushrooms & cheese,” [https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/baked-eggs-potatoes-mushrooms-cheese/](https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/baked-eggs-potatoes-mushrooms-cheese/) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s potato, mushroom, egg and cheese bake as a two-person main meal.
- Uses the catalog’s dried herb blend and four eggs for a filling dinner without adding bread or a raw side.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes into 2 cm chunks and boil for 5 minutes. Drain well.
2. Heat the oil in an ovenproof frying pan. Fry the potatoes for 5 minutes, then add the sliced mushrooms and garlic and cook for 5–8 minutes until browned. Season with the salt, pepper and herbs.
3. Stir through half the cheese. Make four wells and crack in the eggs. Sprinkle over the remaining cheese.
4. Bake for 12–15 minutes, until the whites are set and the yolks are still soft, then serve from the pan.
