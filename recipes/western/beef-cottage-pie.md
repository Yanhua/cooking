**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 55 minutes

## Ingredients

- 400 g beef mince
- 600 g potatoes
- 0.5 each brown onion, diced
- 1 carrot, diced
- 100 g frozen peas
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 300 ml chicken stock
- 60 ml cooking cream
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Barney Desmazery, “No-fuss shepherd’s pie,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/no-fuss-shepherds-pie](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/no-fuss-shepherds-pie) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Converts Desmazery’s lamb shepherd’s pie into a two-person beef cottage pie, using the source’s onion-carrot filling, tomato purée, Worcestershire and potato topping.
- Shortens the filling simmer for the smaller batch and uses cooking cream rather than butter and milk in the mash; it remains an oven-finished comfort dish.

## Method

1. Peel and chunk the potatoes. Boil for 12–15 minutes until tender, drain, mash with the cream and season with half the salt and pepper.
2. Heat the oil in a frying pan. Cook the onion and carrot for 5 minutes, then add the beef and brown for another 5 minutes, breaking it up.
3. Stir in the tomato paste and Worcestershire sauce for 1 minute. Add the stock and simmer uncovered for 8–10 minutes until thickened. Stir in the peas for the last 2 minutes.
4. Heat the oven to 220°C. Spoon the filling into a small ovenproof dish, top with the mash and roughen the surface with a fork.
5. Bake for 15 minutes until bubbling and lightly browned. Rest for 5 minutes before serving.
