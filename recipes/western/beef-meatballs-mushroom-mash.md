**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g beef mince
- 450 g potatoes
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced
- 0.5 each brown onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic
- 20 g Parmesan
- 100 ml cooking cream
- 75 ml chicken stock
- 150 g frozen peas
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Cassie Best, “Meatballs with creamy mushrooms & mash,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/meatballs-creamy-mushrooms-mash](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/meatballs-creamy-mushrooms-mash) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Best’s meatballs, creamy mushroom sauce, mash and green-vegetable plate to two.
- Uses beef mince and dried Italian herbs instead of the source’s pork or turkey, grated apple and fresh thyme; cooking cream and a little stock replace crème fraîche.

## Method

1. Boil the potatoes for 15 minutes until tender. Drain, mash with 30 ml of the cream and season with a pinch of salt, then keep warm.
2. Mix the beef, Parmesan, herbs, half the salt and half the pepper. Shape into 10–12 small meatballs.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide frying pan. Brown the meatballs for 6–8 minutes, turning, until cooked through. Transfer to a plate.
4. Add the remaining oil and cook the onion for 4 minutes. Add the garlic and mushrooms and cook for 5 minutes until softened and browned.
5. Add the stock and remaining cream and simmer for 3 minutes. Return the meatballs and simmer for 2 minutes until hot. Boil the peas separately for 3 minutes, then serve with the mash.
