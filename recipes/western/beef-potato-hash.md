**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g beef mince
- 450 g potatoes, diced
- 0.5 each brown onion, diced
- 250 g fresh tomatoes, chopped
- 60 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 100 g frozen peas
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Betty Crocker Kitchens, “Hamburger Hash Skillet Supper,” [https://www.bettycrocker.com/recipes/hamburger-hash-skillet-supper/fea3f6be-0d92-40d1-94cc-209e690953cb](https://www.bettycrocker.com/recipes/hamburger-hash-skillet-supper/fea3f6be-0d92-40d1-94cc-209e690953cb) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s one-pan beef-and-potato hash with tomato, cream and Worcestershire sauce.
- Uses fresh parboiled potatoes instead of refrigerated diced potatoes with onion and adds peas for a two-person dinner; the onion is cooked until soft.

## Method

1. Dice the potatoes into 1.5 cm pieces and boil for 8 minutes until nearly tender. Drain well.
2. Heat the oil in a large frying pan. Cook the onion for 3 minutes, add the beef and brown for 5–6 minutes, breaking it up.
3. Add the potatoes and fry for 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the edges are golden.
4. Stir in the tomatoes, cream, Worcestershire sauce and peas. Simmer for 4–5 minutes until the peas are hot and the sauce coats the hash.
5. Season with the salt and pepper and serve hot.
