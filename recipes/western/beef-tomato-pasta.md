**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g beef mince
- 250 g dried pasta
- 1 each zucchini
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 0.5 each brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 30 g Parmesan

## Method

1. Cook pasta according to the packet.
2. Finely chop onion and dice zucchini. Brown beef in oil for 5 minutes.
3. Add onion and zucchini for 5 minutes, then minced garlic and herbs.
4. Add tomatoes and simmer for 8 minutes until beef is cooked. Toss with pasta and finish with Parmesan.
