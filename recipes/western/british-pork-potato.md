**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 400 g potatoes
- 150 g frozen peas
- 0.5 each brown onion
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Dice potatoes into 1.5 cm pieces and boil for 10–12 minutes until tender, then drain.
2. Chop onion. Brown pork and onion in oil for 6–8 minutes until pork is cooked.
3. Add potatoes and fry for 4 minutes.
4. Stir in peas, mustard and 3 tbsp water. Cover and cook for 3–4 minutes until peas are hot. Season and serve.
