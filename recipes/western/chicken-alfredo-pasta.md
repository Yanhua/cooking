**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g boneless chicken thighs
- 200 g dried pasta
- 200 ml cooking cream
- 50 g Parmesan
- 100 g frozen peas
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1/4 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Elena Silcock, “Chicken Alfredo,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chicken-alfredo](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chicken-alfredo) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Silcock's chicken Alfredo to two and keeps pan-cooked chicken, creamy Parmesan sauce and pasta-water finish.
- Adds frozen peas, one of the source's suggested additions; boneless thighs are cooked through before slicing.

## Method

1. Cook chicken in oil until browned and cooked through, then slice or shred it.
2. Boil pasta until just tender and reserve cooking water.
3. Simmer cream and Parmesan until smooth; add peas or spinach until warmed or wilted.
4. Toss in pasta and chicken, loosen with cooking water, and season with pepper.
