**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, thinly sliced
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 1/2 brown onion, sliced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 lime
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 4 small flour tortillas

## Source

- Primary: Steven Morris, “Easy Chicken Fajitas,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/easy-chicken-fajitas](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/easy-chicken-fajitas) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Morris's easy chicken fajitas to two and keeps paprika, cumin, garlic, lime and hot-pan char.
- Cooks onion and capsicum fully and omits raw salad and fresh salsa to suit the household.

## Method

1. Heat oven to 180°C and wrap tortillas in foil to warm.
2. Mix paprika, cumin, garlic, oil and lime and coat the boneless chicken thighs with capsicum and onion.
3. Cook everything in a very hot pan until the protein is done and vegetables are charred and tender.
4. Serve hot filling in warm tortillas; omit raw salsa or salad.
