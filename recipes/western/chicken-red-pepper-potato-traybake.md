**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 400 g potatoes
- 1 red capsicum
- 0.5 brown onion
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 lemon
- 100 g plain yoghurt
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Chicken, red pepper & almond traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chicken-red-pepper-almond-traybake/](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chicken-red-pepper-almond-traybake/) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s chicken, potato, red pepper, lemon, cumin and paprika traybake but scales it to two.
- Omits almonds and fennel and serves plain yoghurt alongside; the capsicum and onion are roasted rather than left raw.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes, capsicum and onion into bite-sized pieces. Toss them and the chicken with the garlic, cumin, paprika, lemon zest and juice, oil, salt and pepper.
2. Spread everything in a roasting dish and roast for 35–40 minutes, turning the vegetables once, until the potatoes are tender and the chicken is cooked through.
3. Remove the tray from the oven and rest the chicken for 2 minutes.
4. Spoon the roasting juices over the chicken and potatoes and serve with the yoghurt.
