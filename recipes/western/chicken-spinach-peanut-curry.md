**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-size
- 120 g baby spinach
- 1 1/2 tbsp smooth peanut butter
- 200 ml coconut milk
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp curry powder
- 100 ml water
- 1 lime
- 150 g dry rice
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Chickpea, Spinach & Almond Butter Bowl,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chickpea-spinach-almond-butter-bowl](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chickpea-spinach-almond-butter-bowl) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Good Food's chickpea-spinach bowl as foundation and adds chicken, coconut milk and peanut butter; this is a labelled inspired adaptation.
- Keeps the curry-and-spinach structure and serves hot over rice.

## Method

1. Start the rice. Cook onion, garlic, cumin and curry powder in oil until fragrant.
2. Add chickpeas, coconut milk, peanut butter and water and simmer until thick.
3. Fold in spinach and cook until wilted; add lemon or lime and taste.
4. Serve over rice with peanuts.
