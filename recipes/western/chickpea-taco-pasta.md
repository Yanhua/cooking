**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 240 g chickpeas, drained
- 180 g dried pasta
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 100 g frozen corn
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1/2 brown onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 300 ml water
- 80 g shredded cheese
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Beth Moncel, “Taco Pasta,” [https://www.budgetbytes.com/beef-taco-pasta/](https://www.budgetbytes.com/beef-taco-pasta/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Moncel's suggested bean alternative to drained chickpeas and scales the one-pot recipe to two.
- Keeps tomato, corn, zucchini, cumin, paprika, chilli and cheese; chickpeas are simmered until hot.

## Method

1. Cook onion and garlic in oil in a deep skillet until softened.
2. Add the chickpeas, cumin, paprika and chilli and cook until browned.
3. Stir in tomatoes, corn, pasta and water. Cover and simmer, stirring often, until pasta is tender.
4. Fold through cheese, taste for seasoning, and serve hot.
