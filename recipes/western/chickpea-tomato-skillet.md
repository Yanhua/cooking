**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 480 g chickpeas (drained)
- 1 each zucchini
- 1 each red capsicum
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice. Dice zucchini and capsicum and drain chickpeas.
2. Fry vegetables in oil for 5 minutes.
3. Add minced garlic and paprika for 30 seconds. Stir in tomatoes and chickpeas.
4. Simmer for 10 minutes until vegetables are tender. Serve with rice.
