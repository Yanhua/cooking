**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 250 g dried pasta
- 1 each broccoli
- 125 g mushrooms
- 3 cloves garlic
- 200 ml cooking cream
- 35 g Parmesan
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces and broccoli into small florets. Slice mushrooms and mince garlic.
2. Boil pasta in well-salted water. Add broccoli for the final 3 minutes, then reserve 1/2 cup pasta water and drain.
3. Meanwhile, heat oil in a large frying pan. Season chicken and cook for 5–6 minutes until browned and cooked through. Move it to a plate.
4. Add mushrooms to the pan and cook for 3 minutes. Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds.
5. Add cream, Parmesan, chicken, pasta, and broccoli. Toss for 1–2 minutes, loosening with a little reserved pasta water. Season to taste.
