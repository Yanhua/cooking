**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200 g dried pasta
- 150 g mushrooms
- 120 g baby spinach
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 200 ml cooking cream
- 20 g Parmesan
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Liberty Mendez, “Creamy Mushroom & Spinach Pasta,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/creamy-mushroom-spinach-pasta](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/creamy-mushroom-spinach-pasta) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Mendez's 25-minute pasta for two and keeps mushrooms, spinach, creamy sauce, Parmesan and optional chilli.
- Uses cooking cream in place of crème fraîche and cooks onion fully.

## Method

1. Boil pasta until just tender and reserve cooking water.
2. Cook mushrooms and garlic in oil until browned, then add cream and simmer briefly.
3. Stir in spinach, Parmesan, chilli and pepper.
4. Toss through pasta, loosening with cooking water, and serve.
