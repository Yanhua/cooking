**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g salmon fillets
- 350 g potatoes
- 0.5 brown onion
- 1 clove garlic
- 100 g frozen peas
- 120 ml cooking cream
- 75 ml chicken stock
- 0.5 lemon
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Esther Clark, “Creamy salmon, leek & potato traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/creamy-salmon-leek-potato-traybake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/creamy-salmon-leek-potato-traybake) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s creamy salmon and potato traybake format and scales it to two.
- Uses cooked onion instead of leek, peas instead of capers, lemon for brightness and cooking cream for the sauce.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes into thin wedges, toss with 1 tbsp oil, salt and pepper, and roast for 20 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, cook the sliced onion in the remaining oil for 5 minutes. Add the garlic, cream, stock, lemon zest and peas and simmer for 3 minutes.
3. Pour the cream mixture over the potatoes. Season the salmon, place it on top and bake for 8–10 minutes, or grill briefly, until the salmon is just cooked.
4. Rest for 2 minutes and serve with the creamy potatoes.
