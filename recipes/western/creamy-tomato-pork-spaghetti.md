# Creamy tomato pork and mushroom spaghetti

**Serves:** 2  
**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 200–250 g dried spaghetti
- 125 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1 small brown onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 can (400 g) crushed tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 100 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs or oregano
- Salt, black pepper, and Parmesan to serve

## Method

1. Cook spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.
2. Heat oil in a large frying pan. Cook pork mince for 4–5 minutes, breaking it up until browned.
3. Add onion and mushrooms; cook for 3 minutes. Add garlic, tomato paste, and herbs; stir for 30 seconds.
4. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 8 minutes. Stir in cream, then toss through spaghetti, adding pasta water as needed. Season and top with Parmesan.
