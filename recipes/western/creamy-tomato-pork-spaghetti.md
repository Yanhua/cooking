**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork mince
- 250 g dried spaghetti
- 125 g mushrooms
- 1 each brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 100 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper
- 30 g Parmesan

## Method

1. Finely chop onion and garlic and slice mushrooms.
2. Cook spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water, then drain.
3. Heat oil in a large frying pan. Cook pork mince for 4–5 minutes, breaking it up until browned.
4. Add onion and mushrooms; cook for 3 minutes. Add garlic, tomato paste, and herbs; stir for 30 seconds.
5. Add crushed tomatoes and simmer for 8 minutes. Stir in cream, then toss through spaghetti, adding pasta water as needed. Season and top with Parmesan.
