**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 350 g potatoes
- 100 g baby spinach
- 100 g frozen peas
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Sophie Godwin, “Honey & mustard chicken thighs with spring veg,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/honey-mustard-chicken-thighs-spring-veg?v=1635118462](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/honey-mustard-chicken-thighs-spring-veg?v=1635118462) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales the source’s chicken-thigh traybake to two and uses the catalog’s boneless chicken thighs.
- Uses a larger potato portion and adds spinach with the peas at the end so the vegetables are cooked and the dish needs no raw salad.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Mix the honey, mustard, crushed garlic, lemon zest and juice, salt and pepper. Coat the chicken with half of the mixture.
2. Cut the potatoes into wedges and put them on a baking tray with the chicken. Toss the potatoes with the oil and roast for 35 minutes, turning the potatoes once.
3. Stir the remaining honey-mustard mixture through the tray. Add the peas and spinach for the final 3–4 minutes, until hot and wilted.
4. Rest the chicken for 2 minutes, then serve with the roast potatoes and greens.
