**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g pork loin chops
- 450 g potatoes
- 200 g green beans
- 1 clove garlic
- 0.5 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 lemon
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Irena Macri, “Oven Baked Pork Chops With Honey Mustard Glaze,” [https://www.cookedandloved.com/recipes/oven-baked-pork-chops/](https://www.cookedandloved.com/recipes/oven-baked-pork-chops/) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales the source’s pork, potato and green-bean traybake to two.
- Uses the catalog’s single Dijon mustard and dried paprika, keeping the source’s honey, lemon and garlic glaze.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 210°C. Cut the potatoes into wedges, toss with 1 tbsp oil, salt and pepper, and roast for 10 minutes.
2. Mix the honey, mustard, crushed garlic, paprika and lemon juice. Coat the pork chops with the glaze.
3. Add the chops and beans to the tray. Roast for 15 minutes, then grill for 3–5 minutes until the glaze is browned and the pork is cooked through.
4. Rest the pork for 3 minutes and serve with the potatoes and beans.
