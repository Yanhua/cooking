**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g salmon fillets
- 300 g potatoes
- 1 head broccoli
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Honey mustard salmon, potato and long-stem broccoli traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/honey-mustard-salmon-potato-and-tenderstem-traybake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/honey-mustard-salmon-potato-and-tenderstem-traybake) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s honey-mustard salmon, potato and broccoli traybake and scales it to two.
- Uses ordinary broccoli cut into small florets in place of tenderstem broccoli.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Cut the potatoes into small chunks and boil for 8–10 minutes. Add the broccoli for the final 2 minutes, then drain.
2. Put the potatoes and broccoli in a roasting dish. Mix the honey, mustard and oil, then toss half through the vegetables.
3. Season the salmon with salt and pepper, place it on the tray and brush with the remaining glaze. Roast for 15–20 minutes, until the salmon flakes and the potatoes are browned.
4. Serve immediately with the tray juices.
