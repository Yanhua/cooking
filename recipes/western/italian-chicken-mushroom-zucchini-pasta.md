# Italian chicken, mushroom and zucchini pasta

**Serves:** 2  
**Active time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-sized pieces
- 200–250 g short pasta
- 1 zucchini, sliced into half-moons
- 125 g mushrooms, sliced
- 1/2 brown onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs or oregano
- 40 g finely grated Parmesan, plus more to serve
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt and black pepper

## Method

1. Boil pasta in salted water. Reserve 3/4 cup pasta water, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, heat oil in a large frying pan. Season chicken and cook for 5–6 minutes until browned and cooked through; transfer to a plate.
3. Cook onion, mushrooms, and zucchini in the same pan for 5–6 minutes until soft and lightly browned. Add garlic, tomato paste, and herbs for 30 seconds.
4. Return chicken to the pan with pasta, Parmesan, and 1/2 cup pasta water. Toss for 1–2 minutes, adding more water as needed for a glossy sauce. Season and serve.
