**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 250 g dried pasta
- 1 each zucchini
- 125 g mushrooms
- 0.5 each brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 40 g Parmesan
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Cut chicken into bite-sized pieces. Slice zucchini and mushrooms and finely chop onion and garlic.
2. Boil pasta in salted water. Reserve 3/4 cup pasta water, then drain.
3. Meanwhile, heat oil in a large frying pan. Season chicken and cook for 5–6 minutes until browned and cooked through; transfer to a plate.
4. Cook onion, mushrooms, and zucchini in the same pan for 5–6 minutes until soft and lightly browned. Add garlic, tomato paste, and herbs for 30 seconds.
5. Return chicken to the pan with pasta, Parmesan, and 1/2 cup pasta water. Toss for 1–2 minutes, adding more water as needed for a glossy sauce. Season and serve.
