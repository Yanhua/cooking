**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g salmon fillets, cubed
- 200 g dried pasta
- 1 each broccoli
- 100 g frozen peas
- 1/2 brown onion
- 160 ml cooking cream
- 1 lemon
- 20 g Parmesan
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Caroline Hire, “Pasta with Salmon & Peas,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/pasta-salmon-peas](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/pasta-salmon-peas) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Hire's salmon-and-pea pasta as the foundation and adds broccoli as the cooked green vegetable.
- Uses cream, lemon and Parmesan for the two-person finish; salmon is gently cooked in the sauce.

## Method

1. Boil pasta until just tender; reserve 50 ml cooking water and drain.
2. Cook onion in oil until soft, then add peas, salmon, cream and water.
3. Cook 3–4 minutes until salmon flakes and is cooked through.
4. Stir through pasta with lemon and pepper and serve.
