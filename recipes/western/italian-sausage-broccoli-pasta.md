**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g pork sausages, skins removed
- 200 g dried pasta
- 1 each broccoli
- 180 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 80 g shredded cheese
- 20 g Parmesan
- 2 cloves garlic

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Cheesy Broccoli Pasta Bake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cheesy-broccoli-pasta-bake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cheesy-broccoli-pasta-bake) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Combines Good Food's 30-minute cheesy broccoli bake with the sausage-and-broccoli variation listed in the same recipe collection.
- Uses sausage meat, cooking cream and Parmesan for a grill-finished bake; broccoli cooks with the pasta.

## Method

1. Boil pasta, adding broccoli for the final 4 minutes; drain.
2. Brown sausage meat with garlic and onion, then add cream and mustard.
3. Fold pasta, broccoli, Parmesan and half the cheese through the sauce.
4. Transfer to a dish, top with remaining cheese, and grill until golden.
