**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 200 g dried pasta
- 1 each broccoli
- 180 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 80 g shredded cheese
- 20 g Parmesan
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Cheesy Broccoli Pasta Bake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cheesy-broccoli-pasta-bake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/cheesy-broccoli-pasta-bake) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Good Food's cheesy broccoli bake to browned firm tofu for a vegetarian protein addition.
- Replaces flour-and-milk sauce with cream and pasta water and keeps the mustard-cheese grill finish.

## Method

1. Heat the oven grill to high. Boil pasta, adding broccoli for the final 4 minutes; drain.
2. Heat cream, mustard, Parmesan, half the cheese and a splash of pasta water until smooth.
3. Fold pasta and broccoli through sauce and transfer to an ovenproof dish.
4. Top with remaining cheese and grill 3–4 minutes until bubbling and golden.
