**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 400 g potatoes
- 200 g green beans
- 1 each lemon
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Cut potatoes into 2 cm cubes and boil for 12–15 minutes; add beans for the last 4 minutes, then drain.
2. Meanwhile, slice chicken and fry in oil for 6–8 minutes until cooked through.
3. Add minced garlic and herbs for 30 seconds. Stir in lemon juice and 2 tbsp water.
4. Toss in drained potatoes and beans; season and cook together for 2 minutes.
