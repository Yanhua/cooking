**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 50 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 400 g potatoes
- 200 g green beans
- 0.5 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 lemon
- 1 tsp dried oregano and Italian herbs
- 150 ml chicken stock
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Lemon & oregano chicken traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/lemon-oregano-chicken-traybake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/lemon-oregano-chicken-traybake) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales the source’s traybake to two and uses boneless chicken thighs for a shorter, weeknight-friendly cook.
- Omits bacon and wine, replacing the wine with chicken stock; green beans keep the vegetable component fully cooked.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut the potatoes into small wedges and slice the onion. Toss them with half the oil, half the garlic, salt and pepper in a roasting dish. Roast for 15 minutes.
2. Add the chicken, remaining garlic, lemon zest and juice, herbs and stock. Drizzle with the remaining oil and roast for 20–25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the potatoes are browned.
3. Steam or microwave the beans for 4–5 minutes, then stir them through the tray juices.
4. Rest the chicken for 2 minutes and serve with the potatoes and beans.
