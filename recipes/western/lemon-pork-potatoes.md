**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g pork loin, thick slices
- 500 g potatoes, wedges
- 200 g green beans
- 1 lemon
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 60 ml water
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Quick Pork with Mild Spices,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/quick-pork-mild-spices](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/quick-pork-mild-spices) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Good Food's quick pork pan's short sear and spice discipline into a lemon-potato tray dinner.
- Uses pork loin with potatoes and green beans; all produce is cooked.

## Method

1. Heat oven to 220°C. Toss potatoes with oil, lemon, garlic, herbs, salt and water and roast 15 minutes.
2. Add the pork loin and green beans and roast until the protein is cooked and potatoes are tender.
3. Turn the protein once and spoon over the lemony pan juices.
4. Rest 2 minutes and serve hot.
