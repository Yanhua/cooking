**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 160 g brown lentils, rinsed
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 120 g baby spinach
- 1 carrot, diced
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
- 500 ml chicken stock
- 1 lemon
- 100 g dry rice
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Chickpea, Tomato & Spinach Curry,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chickpea-tomato-spinach-curry](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chickpea-tomato-spinach-curry) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Good Food's chickpea-tomato-spinach curry to two and makes brown lentils the main pulse.
- Keeps tomato, ginger, cumin, chilli, greens and lemon; uses stock and rice for a complete dinner.

## Method

1. Rinse lentils. Cook onion, carrot, garlic, cumin and paprika in oil until softened.
2. Add tomatoes, lentils and stock. Cover and simmer until lentils are tender.
3. Fold in spinach for the final 2 minutes and season with lemon, salt and pepper.
4. Serve as a thick stew with rice.
