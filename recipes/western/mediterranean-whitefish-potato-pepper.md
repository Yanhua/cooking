**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g boneless white fish fillets
- 300 g potatoes
- 1 red capsicum
- 50 g pitted black olives
- 0.5 lemon
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Angela Boggiano, “Roast sea bass & vegetable traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/roast-sea-bass-vegetable-traybake/](https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/roast-sea-bass-vegetable-traybake/) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s staged roast of potatoes, red pepper, olives, lemon and fish, scaled to two.
- Uses a mild boneless white fish and dried Italian herbs in place of sea bass, fresh rosemary and basil.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 180°C. Slice the potatoes thinly and cut the capsicum into strips. Toss them with 1 tbsp oil, herbs, salt and pepper and roast for 25 minutes.
2. Place the fish over the vegetables. Add the olives and lemon slices, drizzle with the remaining oil and roast for 8–10 minutes, until the fish flakes easily.
3. Remove the tray from the oven and rest the fish for 2 minutes.
4. Spoon the pan juices over the fish and vegetables before serving.
