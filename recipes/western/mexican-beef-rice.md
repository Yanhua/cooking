**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 250 g beef mince
- 150 g frozen corn
- 1 each red capsicum
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 50 g shredded cheese
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Method

1. Start rice and dice capsicum.
2. Brown beef in oil for 5–6 minutes.
3. Add capsicum and spices; cook for 3 minutes. Stir in tomatoes and corn.
4. Simmer for 8 minutes until beef is cooked and capsicum is tender. Serve over rice topped with cheese.
