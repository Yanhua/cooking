**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200 g pork mince
- 1 each zucchini
- 1 each red capsicum
- 0.5 each brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 150 g frozen corn
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 0.5 tsp chilli flakes
- 100 g shredded cheese
- 150 g dry rice
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Method

1. Dice zucchini and capsicum and finely chop onion and garlic.
2. Start the measured dry rice in the rice cooker according to its instructions.
3. Heat a large frying pan with oil. Brown pork for 3–4 minutes, then add onion, zucchini, capsicum, and garlic; cook for 5 minutes until softened.
4. Stir in tomato paste and spices for 30 seconds. Add tomatoes and corn, then simmer for 8–10 minutes until thick.
5. Top with cheese, cover for 1–2 minutes until melted, and serve over rice. All vegetables are cooked; no raw garnish is required.
