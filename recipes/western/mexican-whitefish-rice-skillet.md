**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g white fish fillets
- 150 g dry rice
- 200 g canned tomatoes
- 100 g frozen corn
- 1 red capsicum
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 250 ml water

## Source

- Primary: Sophie Godwin, “Blackened Salmon Fajitas,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/blackened-salmon-fajitas](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/blackened-salmon-fajitas) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Godwin's blackened-salmon fajita spice profile as the foundation for white fish.
- Moves cooked fish into a tomato-corn-capsicum rice skillet and omits avocado/raw salsa; fish is added only at the end.

## Method

1. Start rice. Season fish with paprika, cumin, salt and pepper.
2. Sear fish in oil until just cooked and transfer out.
3. Cook onion, capsicum and garlic, then add tomatoes and corn and simmer until hot.
4. Return fish briefly to warm through; serve over rice with lemon.
