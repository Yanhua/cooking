**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs, bite-size
- 240 g chickpeas, drained
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 250 ml chicken stock
- 1/2 brown onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 cinnamon stick
- 10 g ginger
- 150 g dry rice
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Chelsie Collins, “Easy Chicken & Chickpea Tagine,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chicken-chickpea-tagine/](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chicken-chickpea-tagine/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Collins's chicken-and-chickpea tagine and shortens the simmer by using small boneless thigh pieces.
- Keeps tomato, chickpeas, cumin and cinnamon, replaces couscous with rice, and omits raw herbs/fruit.

## Method

1. Start rice. Cook onion, garlic, cumin, paprika and cinnamon in oil until fragrant.
2. Add chicken and brown the outside.
3. Stir in chickpeas, tomatoes and stock, cover and simmer until chicken is cooked and sauce thickens.
4. Taste and serve over rice.
