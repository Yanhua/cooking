**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g mushrooms, finely chopped
- 200 g dried pasta
- 1/2 brown onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 150 ml chicken stock
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 30 g Parmesan
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Chef JB, “Mushroom Ragu Pasta,” [https://www.recipetineats.com/mushroom-ragu-pasta-recipe/](https://www.recipetineats.com/mushroom-ragu-pasta-recipe/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Chef JB's stovetop mushroom ragu to two and retains the browned mushroom base, tomato, stock and Parmesan.
- Uses standard mushrooms and dried herbs instead of porcini, anchovy and wine; the adaptation is disclosed.

## Method

1. Boil pasta until just tender and reserve cooking water.
2. Brown mushrooms, onion and garlic in oil until deeply coloured.
3. Add tomatoes, stock and herbs and simmer until thick and savoury.
4. Toss through pasta, loosen with cooking water, and finish with Parmesan.
