**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 85 minutes

## Ingredients

- 600 g baking potatoes
- 250 g mushrooms
- 100 g baby spinach
- 100 ml cooking cream
- 50 g grated cheese
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Miriam Nice, “Mushroom jacket potatoes,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/mushroom-jacket-potatoes](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/mushroom-jacket-potatoes) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s baked potato with garlicky mushrooms and scales it to two.
- Replaces the sour-cream-and-chive dip with a hot mushroom, spinach and cream filling, finished with cheese so it works as a complete dinner.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Prick the potatoes, rub with half the oil and bake for 70–75 minutes, until tender all the way through.
2. Heat the remaining oil in a frying pan. Cook the sliced mushrooms for 5–7 minutes until browned, then add the garlic for 30 seconds.
3. Add the spinach and cream and cook for 2–3 minutes until the spinach wilts. Season with the salt and pepper.
4. Split the potatoes, spoon in the mushroom mixture, top with cheese and return to the oven for 3–5 minutes to melt. Serve hot.
