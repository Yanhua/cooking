**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g beef rump steak
- 200 g mushrooms
- 1/2 brown onion
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 180 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 120 ml chicken stock
- 150 g dry rice
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Barney Desmazery, “Best Ever Beef Stroganoff,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/best-ever-beef-stroganoff](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/best-ever-beef-stroganoff) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Adapts Desmazery's beef stroganoff to a two-person faster stovetop version and keeps steak, mushrooms, paprika, mustard and creamy sauce.
- Uses cooking cream instead of soured cream and omits porcini; thin rump steak is sliced after searing and served with rice.

## Method

1. Start rice or boil potatoes. Cook the beef rump steak in oil until browned and cooked; transfer out.
2. Cook onion, garlic and mushrooms until soft.
3. Stir mustard, cream and stock into the pan and simmer until slightly thickened.
4. Return protein, warm through, and serve over rice or potatoes.
