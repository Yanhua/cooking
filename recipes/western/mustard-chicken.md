**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 125 g mushrooms
- 100 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 200 g green beans
- 400 g potatoes
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

## Method

1. Cube potatoes and boil for 12–15 minutes. Add beans for the final 4 minutes.
2. Slice chicken and mushrooms. Fry chicken in oil for 6–8 minutes until cooked through.
3. Add mushrooms and cook for 4 minutes. Stir in mustard, cream and a splash of water.
4. Simmer for 2 minutes, season and serve with drained potatoes and beans.
