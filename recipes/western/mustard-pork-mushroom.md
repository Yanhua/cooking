**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g pork loin, thinly sliced
- 1/2 brown onion, sliced
- 1 clove garlic
- 150 g mushrooms
- 150 ml cooking cream
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 15 g Thai basil, torn
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Phil Vickery, “Creamy Mustard Pork,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/creamy-mustard-pork](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/creamy-mustard-pork) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Vickery's 25-minute creamy mustard pork to two and keeps pork, onion, mushroom, mustard and creamy finish.
- Uses cooking cream in place of crème fraîche and serves with rice; onion is cooked soft.

## Method

1. Start rice or boil potatoes. Cook the pork loin in oil until browned and cooked; transfer out.
2. Cook onion, garlic and mushrooms until soft.
3. Stir mustard, cream and stock into the pan and simmer until slightly thickened.
4. Return protein, warm through, and serve over rice or potatoes.
