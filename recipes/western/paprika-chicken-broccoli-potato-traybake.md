**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 60 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 300 g potatoes
- 1 head broccoli
- 1 red capsicum
- 0.5 brown onion
- 3 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1 lemon
- 200 ml chicken stock
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Sara Buenfeld, “Roast chicken traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/roast-chicken-tray-bake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/roast-chicken-tray-bake) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s staged roast of chicken, potato, capsicum, onion and broccoli, scaled to two.
- Uses lemon in place of the source’s lime and dried Italian herbs in place of fresh thyme; boneless chicken keeps the timing practical.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes, capsicum and onion into small pieces and toss with half the oil, half the garlic, paprika, herbs, salt and pepper. Roast for 15 minutes.
2. Add the chicken rubbed with the remaining oil, garlic, lemon zest and juice. Roast for 30 minutes, turning the potatoes once.
3. Add the broccoli florets and pour the stock around the vegetables. Cover loosely with foil and roast for 10 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the broccoli is tender.
4. Rest for 2 minutes, then serve with the tray juices.
