**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g pork loin chops
- 240 g drained canned chickpeas
- 120 g baby spinach
- 0.5 brown onion
- 1 clove garlic
- 100 ml chicken stock
- 60 ml cooking cream
- 0.5 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Esther Clark, “Pork chops & mustardy butter beans,” [https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/pork-chops-mustardy-butter-beans/](https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/pork-chops-mustardy-butter-beans/) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s pan-seared pork with creamy mustardy beans and spinach, scaled to two.
- Uses canned chickpeas in place of butter beans and cooking cream in place of crème fraîche; the onion is softened before serving.

## Method

1. Heat 0.5 tbsp oil in a large frying pan. Cook the sliced onion for 8–10 minutes until soft and lightly golden. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
2. Stir in the chickpeas and stock. Add the spinach and cook for 3 minutes until wilted. Mash about one-third of the chickpeas against the pan.
3. Stir in the cream and mustard and simmer for 3 minutes. Season with half the salt and pepper, then keep warm.
4. Season the pork with the remaining salt and pepper. Heat the remaining oil in a second pan and sear the chops for 6–7 minutes per side, until cooked through. Rest for 3 minutes and serve over the chickpeas.
