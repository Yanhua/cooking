**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 55 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g pork loin chops
- 400 g potatoes
- 250 g tomatoes
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 tsp dried rosemary and Italian herbs
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “How to cook pork chops,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/howto/guide/how-cook-pork-chops](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/howto/guide/how-cook-pork-chops) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses the source’s oven method of parboiled potatoes, tomatoes and pork chops, scaled to two.
- Uses the catalog’s pork loin chops and dried rosemary-herb blend for a straightforward supermarket version.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes into thin wedges and boil for 6–8 minutes. Drain.
2. Layer the potatoes and sliced tomatoes in a roasting dish with the garlic, herbs, half the oil, salt and pepper. Bake for 15 minutes.
3. Rub the pork chops with the remaining oil and season. Set them over the potatoes and roast for 30–35 minutes, turning once, until cooked through.
4. Rest the chops for 3 minutes, then serve with the tomato potatoes.
