**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g raw prawns, peeled
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 1/2 brown onion, sliced
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 lime
- 100 g frozen corn
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 4 small flour tortillas

## Source

- Primary: Steven Morris, “Easy Chicken Fajitas,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/easy-chicken-fajitas](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/easy-chicken-fajitas) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses the Good Food fajita marinade and hot-pan format with prawns, a source-supported fajita protein family.
- Cooks capsicum, onion and corn and omits raw salad or salsa; prawns are removed as soon as opaque.

## Method

1. Heat oven to 180°C and wrap tortillas in foil to warm.
2. Mix paprika, cumin, garlic, oil and lime and coat the raw prawns with capsicum and onion.
3. Cook everything in a very hot pan until the protein is done and vegetables are charred and tender.
4. Serve hot filling in warm tortillas; omit raw salsa or salad.
