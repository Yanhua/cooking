**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g beef rump steak, thick strips
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 225 ml chicken stock
- 100 g frozen peas
- 50 g Greek yoghurt
- 1 1/2 tbsp cooking oil
- 150 g dry rice

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Quick Pork with Mild Spices,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/quick-pork-mild-spices](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/quick-pork-mild-spices) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses the source's paprika-cumin reduction as an inspired beef variation; thin rump steak keeps timing short.
- Keeps peas and yoghurt and serves with rice; dairy is stirred in off heat.

## Method

1. Start rice or boil potatoes. Slice the protein and prepare peas.
2. Stir-fry the beef rump steak in oil until browned and cooked; transfer it out.
3. Bloom paprika and cumin in the pan, add stock and reduce by half.
4. Stir in peas and yoghurt, return protein, warm through, and serve.
