**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 200 g dried pasta
- 1/2 brown onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 carrot, diced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 150 g mushrooms
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 100 g baby spinach
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
- 25 g Parmesan
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Beth Moncel, “Quick Veggie Pasta,” [https://www.budgetbytes.com/quick-veggie-pasta/](https://www.budgetbytes.com/quick-veggie-pasta/) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Moncel's flexible quick veggie pasta to two and keeps the hard-vegetable-first cooking sequence.
- Uses zucchini, carrot, mushroom, spinach and canned tomato; no raw vegetable topping is added.

## Method

1. Boil pasta until just tender and reserve some cooking water.
2. Cook onion, garlic, carrot, zucchini and mushrooms in oil until softened.
3. Add vegetables and tomatoes with herbs and simmer until saucy.
4. Toss in pasta and spinach, loosening with cooking water; finish with Parmesan.
