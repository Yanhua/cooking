**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 50 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g boneless chicken thighs
- 450 g potatoes
- 200 g green beans
- 4 cloves garlic
- 1 lemon
- 1.5 tsp dried rosemary and Italian herbs
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Barney Desmazery, “Rosemary roast chicken thighs, new potatoes, asparagus & garlic,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/rosemary-roast-chicken-thighs-new-potatoes-asparagus-garlic](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/rosemary-roast-chicken-thighs-new-potatoes-asparagus-garlic) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales the source’s roast chicken dish to two and uses boneless chicken thighs.
- Replaces asparagus with green beans and fresh rosemary with the catalog’s dried herb blend while keeping the lemon-garlic roast format.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes into wedges and toss them with half the oil, half the crushed garlic, lemon zest, salt and pepper. Roast for 15 minutes.
2. Add the chicken, remaining garlic, herbs, lemon juice and remaining oil. Roast for 25–30 minutes, turning the potatoes once, until the chicken is cooked through.
3. Steam or microwave the beans for 4–5 minutes while the chicken rests.
4. Serve the chicken and potatoes with the beans and spoon over the garlicky tray juices.
