**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g salmon fillets
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp chilli flakes
- 1 lime
- 1 red capsicum, sliced
- 100 g frozen corn
- 4 small flour tortillas
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Sophie Godwin, “Blackened Salmon Fajitas,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/blackened-salmon-fajitas](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/blackened-salmon-fajitas) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Godwin's 15-minute blackened salmon fajitas to two and keeps the spice-coated pan-fry.
- Replaces avocado with cooked corn and omits raw salsa; tortillas, capsicum and lime remain.

## Method

1. Heat oven to 180°C and wrap tortillas in foil to warm.
2. Coat salmon with oil, paprika, cumin, chilli and lime.
3. Pan-fry salmon 3–4 minutes per side until blackened and just cooked; cook capsicum and corn in the same pan.
4. Flake salmon into warm tortillas with the cooked vegetables and serve with lime.
