**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 20 minutes
**Total time:** 20 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g pork sausages, skins removed
- 125 g mushrooms
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 200 g dried pasta
- 25 g Parmesan
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1/2 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Quick Sausage Bolognese,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/quick-sausage-bolognese](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/quick-sausage-bolognese) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Good Food's quick sausage bolognese to two and keeps sausage meat, mushrooms, tomato sauce and Parmesan.
- Uses 300 g sausage meat and canned tomatoes rather than jarred sauce, with a short simmer.

## Method

1. Boil pasta until just tender.
2. Remove sausage meat from casings and cook in a frying pan with mushrooms until browned.
3. Add tomatoes and herbs and simmer until reduced.
4. Toss pasta through the sauce and finish with Parmesan.
