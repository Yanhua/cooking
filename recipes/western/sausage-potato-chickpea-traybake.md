**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 50 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g pork sausages
- 400 g potatoes
- 0.5 brown onion
- 200 g tomatoes
- 240 g drained canned chickpeas
- 1 lemon
- 150 ml chicken stock
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Esther Clark, “Sausage traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/pesto-sausage-traybake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/pesto-sausage-traybake) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales the source’s sausage and potato traybake to two.
- Uses chickpeas, tomatoes and dried herbs instead of artichokes, spring onions and pesto, with stock added for a simple saucy finish.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes into wedges and the onion into thick slices. Put them in a roasting dish with the sausages, tomatoes, lemon wedges, oil, herbs, salt and pepper.
2. Roast for 25 minutes, turning the potatoes and sausages once.
3. Stir in the drained chickpeas and stock. Roast for a further 15–20 minutes, until the potatoes are tender and the sausages are cooked through.
4. Squeeze the roasted lemon over the tray and serve.
