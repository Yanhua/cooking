**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 25 minutes

## Ingredients

- 240 g chickpeas, drained
- 1 eggplant, diced
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 100 g baby spinach
- 1/2 brown onion
- 2 cloves garlic
- 15 g ginger
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/4 tsp chilli flakes
- 100 ml coconut milk
- 1 lemon
- 150 g dry rice
- 1 tbsp cooking oil

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Chickpea, Tomato & Spinach Curry,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chickpea-tomato-spinach-curry](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/chickpea-tomato-spinach-curry) (accessed 12 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Good Food's chickpea-tomato-spinach curry as the foundation and adds eggplant with smoked paprika.
- Scales it to two, keeps coconut and spinach in the sauce, and serves with rice; every vegetable is cooked.

## Method

1. Start rice. Brown eggplant in oil until softened.
2. Add onion, garlic, cumin, paprika and chilli and cook until fragrant.
3. Stir in tomatoes, chickpeas and water; cover and simmer until eggplant is tender.
4. Fold in spinach if using, taste, and serve over rice.
