**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g pork sausages
- 450 g potatoes
- 1 red capsicum
- 1 brown onion
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 0.25 tsp chilli flakes
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Ailsa Burt, “Smoky sausage & squash traybake,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/smoky-sausage-squash-traybake](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/smoky-sausage-squash-traybake) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s smoky, sweet sausage traybake format and scales it to two.
- Replaces squash with potatoes and capsicum, and harissa with tomato paste and a small amount of chilli so the ingredients stay in the existing catalog.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 200°C. Cut the potatoes, capsicum and onion into bite-sized pieces. Mix the paprika, cumin, honey, tomato paste, chilli, oil, salt and pepper.
2. Toss the sausages and vegetables with the smoky coating in a roasting dish.
3. Roast for 35–40 minutes, turning everything once, until the potatoes are tender and the sausages are cooked through.
4. Rest for 2 minutes, then serve hot.
