**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g beef rump steak
- 430 g potatoes
- 250 g cabbage, finely sliced
- 0.5 each brown onion, sliced
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 0.5 tsp ground cumin
- 0.5 tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Esther Clark, “Smoky steak with Cajun potatoes & spicy slaw,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/smoky-steak-cajun-potatoes-spicy-slaw](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/smoky-steak-cajun-potatoes-spicy-slaw) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses the source’s parboiled, oven-crisped Cajun potatoes and quick-seared steak with a measured paprika-cumin-chilli blend.
- Replaces the source’s raw cabbage, raw onion, mayonnaise and soured cream slaw with cabbage and onion cooked until tender, avoiding a raw-salad finish.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Boil the potatoes for 5 minutes, drain and steam-dry, then toss with the paprika, cumin, chilli, 1 tbsp oil, half the salt and half the pepper.
2. Spread the potatoes on a baking tray and roast for 25–30 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp and golden.
3. Pat the steak dry and season with the remaining salt and pepper. Sear in a very hot frying pan for about 2–4 minutes per side, depending on thickness and preferred doneness. Rest for 5 minutes.
4. While the steak rests, heat the remaining oil in a frying pan. Cook the onion, cabbage and garlic for 5–7 minutes, adding a splash of water if needed, until soft and fully cooked.
5. Slice the steak and serve with the Cajun potatoes and warm cabbage.
