**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 30 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g beef rump steak
- 450 g potatoes
- 2 cloves garlic, lightly crushed
- 0.5 each brown onion, finely sliced
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 150 ml chicken stock
- 60 ml cooking cream
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1 tsp coarse black pepper
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt

## Source

- Primary: The Hairy Bikers, “Steak Diane with sauté potatoes,” [https://git.macropus.org/bbc-food/www.bbc.co.uk/food/recipes/steak_diane_with_saut_67797.html](https://git.macropus.org/bbc-food/www.bbc.co.uk/food/recipes/steak_diane_with_saut_67797.html) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Follows the archived BBC Food recipe’s parboil-then-sauté potato technique and quick steak-and-pan-sauce sequence.
- Replaces butter, brandy, fresh thyme and tarragon with cooking oil, stock and dried Italian herbs; the mustard, Worcestershire and cream sauce remains the Diane-style finish.

## Method

1. Cut the potatoes into 2.5 cm chunks and simmer in salted water for 5 minutes. Drain and shake the colander gently to roughen the edges.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large frying pan. Fry the potatoes over medium heat for 10 minutes, turning occasionally, then add the garlic and herbs and cook for another 5 minutes until golden.
3. Season the steaks with the salt and half the pepper. Heat the remaining oil in a second pan, or move the potatoes to a covered plate, and sear the steaks for 2–3 minutes per side for medium-rare or longer to preference. Rest on a board.
4. Cook the onion in the steak pan for 2–3 minutes. Stir in the Worcestershire sauce, mustard and remaining pepper, then add the stock and reduce by about half.
5. Stir in the cream and simmer until the sauce lightly coats a spoon. Slice the steaks and serve with the sauce and sauté potatoes.
