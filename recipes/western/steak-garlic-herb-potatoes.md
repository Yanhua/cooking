**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g beef rump steak
- 450 g potatoes
- 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
- 2 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 0.5 each lemon
- 0.25 tsp chilli flakes
- 2 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Emma Lewis, “Charred steak with chimichurri sauce,” [https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/charred-steak-with-chimichurri-sauce/](https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/charred-steak-with-chimichurri-sauce/) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Uses Lewis’s charred steak, sharp herb sauce and short resting method as the foundation, with a cooked potato side for a complete dinner.
- This is a deliberate pantry adaptation rather than a traditional chimichurri: dried Italian herbs replace the source’s fresh coriander and parsley, balsamic replaces white-wine vinegar, and raw onion is not used.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut the potatoes into wedges, toss with 1 tbsp oil, half the salt and pepper, and roast for 25–30 minutes until crisp.
2. Stir together the garlic, herbs, balsamic vinegar, juice of the lemon half, chilli flakes and 0.5 tbsp oil. Set the sharp herb dressing aside.
3. Rub the steaks with the remaining oil and season with the remaining salt and pepper. Sear in a very hot pan for 3–4 minutes per side, or to preference, then rest for 5 minutes.
4. Slice the steaks across the grain and spoon the garlic-herb dressing over them. Serve with the roast potatoes.
