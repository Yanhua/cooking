**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 30 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g beef rump steak
- 400 g potatoes
- 100 g frozen peas
- 2 cloves garlic
- 0.5 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Tom Kerridge, “Rib-eye with steak pan potatoes & peas,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/rib-eye-steak-pan-potatoes-peas](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/rib-eye-steak-pan-potatoes-peas) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Kerridge’s four-serving rib-eye dinner to two and uses the catalog’s rump steak.
- Uses balsamic vinegar in place of the source’s red wine vinegar and cooking oil in place of butter; the steak-pan potatoes and peas remain the defining format.

## Method

1. Cut the potatoes into 2 cm chunks and boil for 8–10 minutes until just tender. Drain and leave to steam-dry.
2. Mix the garlic, balsamic vinegar, 0.5 tbsp of the oil and half the pepper. Coat the steaks and leave them while the potatoes cook.
3. Heat a large frying pan until very hot. Sear the steaks for about 3–4 minutes per side, or to the preferred doneness, then transfer to a board to rest.
4. Add the remaining oil and potatoes to the steak pan. Fry until browned, then add the peas and 2 tbsp water for 2 minutes. Season with the salt and remaining pepper.
5. Slice the rested steaks and serve with the browned potatoes and peas.
