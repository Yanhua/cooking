**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 40 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g beef rump steak
- 400 g potatoes
- 200 g mushrooms, sliced
- 0.5 each brown onion, finely chopped
- 1 clove garlic
- 1 tsp coarse black pepper
- 100 ml cooking cream
- 150 ml chicken stock
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Fillet of beef with mixed peppercorn sauce,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/fillet-beef-mixed-peppercorn-sauce](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/fillet-beef-mixed-peppercorn-sauce) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s flash-fried steak, mushrooms, crushed pepper and reduced creamy pan sauce while using rump steak and a two-person quantity.
- Omits brandy and red wine, using stock as the pan reduction, and adds quick roast potatoes as the cooked side instead of the source’s optional accompaniments.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut the potatoes into small wedges, toss with 0.75 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt, and roast for 25–30 minutes until golden.
2. Season the steaks with half the pepper and salt. Heat a large frying pan until very hot, add 0.5 tbsp oil and sear the steaks for 2–4 minutes per side. Transfer to a board to rest.
3. Add the remaining oil to the pan. Cook the onion and mushrooms for 5 minutes until browned, then add the garlic and remaining pepper for 30 seconds.
4. Pour in the stock and reduce for 3–4 minutes. Stir in the cream and simmer for 2 minutes until lightly thickened.
5. Slice the rested steaks and spoon the mushroom-pepper cream over them. Serve with the roast potatoes.
