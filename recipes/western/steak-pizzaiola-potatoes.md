**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 25 minutes
**Total time:** 35 minutes

## Ingredients

- 350 g beef rump steak
- 400 g canned tomatoes
- 400 g potatoes
- 1 clove garlic, roughly chopped
- 1 tsp dried Italian herbs
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Ursula Ferrigno, “Sirloin steaks with pizzaiola sauce,” [https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/sirloin-steaks-with-pizzaiola-sauce/](https://www.bbcgoodfoodme.com/recipes/sirloin-steaks-with-pizzaiola-sauce/) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Scales Ferrigno’s Italian tomato-garlic-oregano steak sauce to two and uses the catalog’s rump steak.
- Omits the source’s rocket garnish and uses boiled potatoes instead of an unspecified new-potato quantity so the whole meal stays cooked and quantified.

## Method

1. Cut the potatoes into 2 cm pieces and boil for 10–12 minutes until tender. Drain and keep covered.
2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a heavy frying pan with the garlic. Season the steaks and sear them briefly on both sides, working in batches if needed.
3. Return the steaks to the pan, add the canned tomatoes, Italian herbs, salt and pepper, and partially cover. Simmer gently for 8–10 minutes, turning the steaks once, until cooked to preference.
4. Transfer the steaks to a clean board and rest. Increase the heat and reduce the tomato sauce for about 5 minutes.
5. Slice the steak and spoon over the pizzaiola sauce. Serve with the potatoes.
