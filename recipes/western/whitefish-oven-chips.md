**Serves:** 2

**Active time:** 15 minutes
**Total time:** 45 minutes

## Ingredients

- 300 g boneless white fish fillets
- 500 g potatoes
- 30 g breadcrumbs
- 200 g tomatoes
- 1 lemon
- 0.5 tsp dried parsley or Italian herbs
- 1.5 tbsp cooking oil
- 0.5 tsp salt
- 0.5 tsp black pepper

## Source

- Primary: Good Food team, “Oven-baked fish & chips,” [https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/oven-baked-fish-chips](https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/oven-baked-fish-chips) (accessed 13 September 2026).

## Adaptations

- Keeps the source’s crisp breadcrumb fish and oven-chip sequence, scaled to two.
- Uses a mild white fish, fresh tomatoes and dried herbs; the tomatoes are roasted so the meal has no raw salad component.

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 220°C. Cut the potatoes into chips, toss with 1 tbsp oil, salt and pepper, and spread on a tray. Roast for 30 minutes, turning once.
2. Mix the breadcrumbs with the lemon zest, herbs and remaining oil. Put the fish in a small baking dish, season, and press the crumb mixture on top.
3. Add the halved tomatoes to the chip tray and put the fish in the oven for the final 10–12 minutes, until the crumb is golden and the fish flakes.
4. Serve with the roast tomatoes, chips and lemon wedges.
